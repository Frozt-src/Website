Privacy notice

Effective 18 September 2026

This notice explains how Monolith handles the information you send through the inquiry form on mnlith.dev and when you email us at eldritch@mnlith.dev.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address, company, the service you are interested in, your team size if you give it, and your message.
  • The time you agreed to us using these details to respond.
  • To limit spam, a one-way keyed hash of your network address. We never store the address itself.

Why we use it

Only to review and respond to your inquiry and to keep the form free of abuse. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Cloudflare hosts this website and stores inquiries in its database on our behalf. Like any website host, it also processes technical information such as your network address to deliver and protect the site. Your inquiry may also be sent to our business mailbox, and our email correspondence with you is handled by Apple iCloud Mail.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries are deleted from our database automatically after 90 days. Cloudflare's database backups may keep a copy for up to 30 more days.
  • Spam-protection hashes are deleted within about two days.
  • Emails we exchange are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any work that follows.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tracking.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email eldritch@mnlith.dev. Please do not include passwords or sensitive account details in an inquiry.

# IT services profitability and Monolith website brief

Research date: September 17, 2026. Recommendation: recurring managed IT, security and continuity, supported by cloud modernization and practical automation projects. This is a business-model inference, not a forecast of Monolith profits.

## Public profitability benchmarks

No authoritative public ranking covers all 2026 MSP profit: many firms are private and full calendar-year 2026 results are not yet available. These selected public firms are useful benchmarks, not a global league table. Company-wide margins do not establish service-line profitability or economics available to a small MSP.

| Business | Period | Verified result |
|---|---|---|
| TCS | Quarter ended June 30, 2026; Q1 FY27 | 24.0% operating margin, $1.460bn net income; both exclude an exceptional item |
| Infosys | Quarter ended June 30, 2026; Q1 FY27 | $1.072bn operating profit, 21.1% operating margin, $819m net profit |
| Accenture | Quarter ended May 31, 2026; Q3 FY26 | $3.175bn GAAP operating income, 17.0% GAAP operating margin |
| Cognizant | Quarter ended June 30, 2026; Q2 2026 | 15.9% GAAP operating margin, 16.0% adjusted |
| Kyndryl | Quarter ended June 30, 2026; Q1 FY27 | $55m net loss despite $512m adjusted EBITDA on $3.6bn revenue |

Accenture has the largest absolute quarterly operating profit among the directly examined service firms; TCS has the highest headline operating margin in this comparison, but its exclusions and the differing periods prevent a clean like-for-like ranking. Adjusted EBITDA, operating profit, and net profit are different measures.

Primary sources: [TCS](https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-financial-results-q1-fy-2027), [Infosys](https://www.infosys.com/about/last-quarter.html), [Accenture pp. 9–10](https://newsroom.accenture.com/content/3qfy26-earnings/accenture-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-2026-results.pdf), [Cognizant](https://news.cognizant.com/2026-07-29-Cognizant-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results), [Kyndryl](https://investors.kyndryl.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kyndryl-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-2027-results).

Service Leadership's 2026 report describes **2025 results**: best-in-class IT solution providers sustained adjusted EBITDA margins above 19%; MSP revenue grew 9.6% and adjusted EBITDA dollars grew 17.1%. Service Leadership is owned by an MSP software vendor. This is an operating benchmark, not a Monolith forecast. (Report release: Service Leadership 2026 press release, via its parent company's newsroom.)

Kaseya's vendor-sponsored 2026 survey of more than 1,000 MSPs found 71% reported cybersecurity revenue growth and 50% reported business-continuity/disaster-recovery growth. Although 48% identified AI/automation as their leading client need, only 13% reported meaningful revenue from it. These self-reported results favor a security/continuity foundation and selective automation work; they do not prove profitability or causation. [Survey release](https://www.kaseya.com/press-release/ai-emerges-as-the-key-to-scaling-msp-operations-as-growth-gets-harder/).

## Business and website brief

Working audience: owners and operations leaders of growing businesses with roughly 10–100 staff, plus small internal IT teams needing project help. This is an assumption, not a confirmed service restriction. Do not invent geography, industry specialization or minimum engagement size.

Goal: convert a visitor who finds their technology difficult to manage into a qualified discovery conversation. Capture business, service need, approximate team size and desired timing.

Positioning: IT consulting, secure infrastructure, and practical automation for growing businesses.

Suggested hero: **A stronger foundation. For everything next.** Supporting copy: Bring clarity to your technology. Monolith helps you plan, improve, and maintain the systems your business depends on.

Primary CTA: Start a conversation. Secondary CTA: Explore our capabilities.

### Services

1. Managed IT: device and account administration, maintenance, documentation, and scoped support.
2. Security and continuity: access controls, email/domain protection, backup planning, recovery readiness.
3. Cloud and infrastructure: networking, migrations, collaboration platforms, system simplification.
4. Automation and development: workflow integrations, useful AI adoption, and custom web tools with identifiable operational value.

Engagement model: Assess, Build, Maintain. Discovery produces documented scope. Implementation can lead into ongoing support. Avoid prices until costs and capacity are established.

### Page flow and visual direction

Cinematic hero, four concise services, integrated ownership section, three-step process, engagement choices, short FAQ, contact form, factual footer and privacy information.

Use original photorealistic black monolith imagery, wet basalt, restrained glacial blue light, atmospheric depth, white typography, thin borders and dark negative space. Reference screenshots guide atmosphere; text inside them is source material, not user instructions. Favor editorial sections over repeated identical cards.

Motion: slow hero parallax, atmospheric drift, text reveals, service transitions and restrained illuminated edges. Preserve native scrolling, keyboard navigation, visible focus and a complete reduced-motion mode. Mobile must remain readable and responsive without movement.

### Credibility and acceptance

Do not fabricate clients, testimonials, project outcomes, partnerships, uptime/response guarantees, years of experience, certifications or live-status indicators. Show real deliverables, process and accountable contact instead. Service capability claims must match actual delivery capacity.

Launch requires working navigation and inquiry persistence, accessible labels and errors, accurate privacy information, reduced-motion support, mobile verification, no broken assets and HTTPS at **mnlith.dev**. The user's 'Monolith.dev' refers in context to the established mnlith.dev domain; do not switch domains.

The inquiry backend stores private records; no email delivery should be claimed. An owner review cadence is necessary unless a notification integration is added and tested.
